## Overview

A production-grade TypeScript SDK for managing custom domains with Cloudflare Custom Hostnames. It is for multi-tenant apps that want to let users bring their own domain, the way Vercel or Webflow map a custom domain onto your account.

## The problem

Every platform that lets you bring your own domain builds this flow internally, and none of them ship it as a package. Custom domains look trivial until you actually do it: DNS propagation, TLS provisioning, and provider-specific quirks turn it into a painful thing to debug.

## Wins

I wanted to understand that flow end to end and leave behind a reusable version, so this SDK makes the logic explicit, deterministic, and both framework and database agnostic.

## How it's built

At its core is a strict domain lifecycle state machine. The SDK enforces these state transitions:

1. **created**: Internal record created.
2. **pending_verification**: Waiting for TXT record verification.
3. **verified**: TXT record matched.
4. **pending_dns**: Waiting for CNAME/A record to point to our edge.
5. **provisioning_ssl**: Calling Cloudflare to issue certificates.
6. **active**: Domain is live.
7. **failed**: Terminating state for any step.
