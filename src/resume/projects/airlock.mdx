## Overview

Airlock is a local harness studio: a desktop app where you bring your own model and get the tooling around it. Chat with a model (images included), dictate into any app, transcribe files with timestamps, have text read aloud and generate images. Transcription, speech-to-text, text-to-speech and image generation all run on your device.

It is not tied to coding. It is a general harness with agent-style capabilities, and more integrations are on the way.

## What you can do with it

- **Keep everything local.** Models, speech and images never leave your machine unless you choose otherwise.
- **Expose it when you want.** Forward a local model or tool to other apps or machines, or keep it all on one computer.
- **Automate with workflows and schedules.** Point a lightweight model at a folder of photos and have them sorted into classified folders, for example, without uploading a single file.
- **Manage a local gallery.** Everything you make lands in a private library that never reaches an online model.

## Privacy by default

The assistant sees a short preview of what you mention with `@`. Reading more needs your approval, and every read is logged locally. Online providers are planned and will follow the same rule.

## How it's built

A Tauri shell with a Rust core runs the engines, and a Svelte 5 interface sits on top. Model, voice and engine lists are plain JSON you can extend. The website is a SvelteKit app on Cloudflare. Pre-release, built and tested on Windows only.
