## Overview

Orbit is a PDF toolkit that does everything on your own device and never uploads a thing. It works in the browser, installs as a PWA on mobile with offline caching, and ships as a Tauri desktop app for anyone who would rather have it as a real app.

## The problem

I built Orbit because I did not trust the web for something as simple as merging a PDF. Most online PDF tools upload your file to their server first, and when a document is sensitive that is a real problem: you have no idea whether it is stored, trained on, or leaked.

## Wins

- **Private by default.** Everything runs locally in your browser's sandbox. No uploads, no server, no telemetry on your documents.
- **Fast.** The heavy lifting runs on a WebAssembly engine, so processing feels desktop-class.
- **Works offline.** Fully functional with no connection. Install it as a PWA on mobile, or grab the Tauri desktop build.
- **No limits.** No daily quotas, no file size caps, and no subscriptions. Batch as much as your machine can handle.

It covers the everyday jobs: merge (drag and drop to reorder), split (extract pages, split ranges, or divide into multiple documents), compress while keeping quality, and convert JPG, PNG and HEIC images to PDF.

Nothing here is groundbreaking. It is convenience done properly: a clean UI, sane defaults, and your files staying yours.

## How it's built

The constraints were simple from day one: lightweight, private, and offline. Page work runs on [`pdf-lib`](https://pdf-lib.js.org/) and [`pdfjs-dist`](https://mozilla.github.io/pdf.js/), and heavy operations like compression run on [`qpdf-wasm`](https://github.com/neslinesli93/qpdf-wasm), so they are fast without a server.
