## Overview

Muse is a fashion discovery app for your phone. You swipe through outfits the way you'd swipe through a dating app, save what you like into a digital closet, and the feed gets closer to your taste as it learns your style, colours and fit.

## The problem

Shopping for clothes online means scrolling endless grids that know nothing about you. Muse starts from your preferences instead: a short onboarding asks about style, colours and fit, and every outfit after that is picked and explained against them.

## Wins

**A swipe deck for outfits.** The main screen is a card deck you swipe through, with each outfit's colour palette on the card and a small pill explaining why the AI picked it.

**AI that reads the clothes.** Gemini 2.5 Flash analyses every product image and returns structured attributes (who it's for, style, colours) validated with Zod, so recommendations work from real data instead of free text.

**A closet, not just a feed.** You can keep a personal wardrobe in the app, and outfit pages show the full preview, the AI's confidence score and direct links to buy from the retailer.

## How it's built

A Turborepo monorepo with three apps: the React Native and Expo mobile app (Expo Router, React Query, Dripsy), a Hono API on Node with MongoDB, Redis for caching and Cloudinary for images, and a small Next.js site for admin and marketing. Auth runs on Better Auth with email and Google sign-in.

## What's next

It's an MVP. The core loop works end to end: onboarding, the swipe feed, the closet and AI analysis of imported products.
