Zoven builds risk management for payments: merchant onboarding, transaction monitoring and fraud detection, run by AI agents that work alongside a client's risk team. I joined full time as a software engineer and work on the detection side, for payment gateways and fintechs in India.

- **Mule account detection**: finding accounts that exist to move money for someone else, before they do damage on a client's platform.
- **Adverse media pipelines**: collecting and screening news about a business or the people behind it, so a risk team sees the bad press before it onboards them.
- **PEP and sanctions screening**: checking merchants and their owners against politically exposed person and sanctions lists as part of onboarding.
- **Merchant onboarding risk**: analysis that helps payment gateways decide faster which merchants are safe to take on, so good businesses get through without a long manual review.
- **Transaction risk and fraud detection**: scoring live transactions for clients so suspicious ones get stopped or flagged.
