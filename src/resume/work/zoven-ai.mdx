Zoven builds risk management for payments: merchant onboarding, transaction monitoring and fraud detection, run by AI agents that work alongside a client's risk team. I joined full time as a software engineer and worked on the detection side, for payment gateways and fintechs in India.

- **Name screening**: checking merchants and their owners against sanctions, PEP and adverse-media lists, scoring the matches, and routing real hits to analysts.
- **Adverse media**: collecting and screening news about a business or the people behind it, so a risk team sees the bad press before it onboards them. I owned this end to end.
- **AML**: anti-money-laundering checks across onboarding and monitoring.
- **STR filing**: suspicious transaction report filing, shipped as a beta feature. I owned it end to end.
- **Mule account detection**: finding accounts that exist to move money for someone else, before they do damage on a client's platform.
- **Merchant onboarding risk**: analysis that helps payment gateways decide faster which merchants are safe to take on, so good businesses get through without a long manual review.
- **Transaction monitoring**: contributed to scoring live transactions so suspicious ones get stopped or flagged.
